The Challenge

India does not lack ambition, ideas, or programmes. The larger challenge is turning policy intent into institutions that perform, systems that scale, and outcomes that endure.

I-PAL was created to address that gap. We combine policy insight, industry perspective, ecosystem strategy, implementation discipline, and technology-oriented thinking to help public and institutional actors build stronger entrepreneurial and innovation systems.

We are not a traditional think tank. We are a dynamic policy-to-implementation platform that works where ideas, institutions, and execution meet.

About I-PAL

Built for consequence, not commentary

India Policy Action Lab is a specialized policy action platform focused on strengthening entrepreneurship, innovation, trade, and technology-enabled development ecosystems in India.

We work across the policy cycle — from diagnostic research and strategy to institutional design, implementation support, evaluation, capacity building, and cross-sector coordination.

I-PAL was founded on a simple premise: policy impact depends not only on the quality of ideas, but on the quality of institutions, execution systems, and ecosystem design.

Vision

To build a high-credibility policy action platform that strengthens India's entrepreneurship, innovation, trade, and internationalisation ecosystems through technology-led, institution-focused, and implementation-oriented solutions.

Mission

To provide policy advisory, ecosystem strategy, institutional design, implementation support, evaluation, and capability development across startups, digital infrastructure, financial inclusion, innovation systems, trade, and internationalisation.
